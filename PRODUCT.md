# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One personal administrator who creates and maintains short links for their own work. They return frequently for quick operational tasks: create a link, find an existing one, change its destination or availability, inspect visits, and remove obsolete links with confidence.

## Product Purpose

Shorten is a fast, self-hosted URL shortener and management tool. It turns long destinations into durable, memorable links and gives one administrator complete lifecycle control without the weight of a multi-user platform. Success means redirects feel instantaneous, management actions are obvious and reversible where possible, and the state shown in the interface can be trusted.

## Brand Personality

Tech, minimal, modern. The product should feel precise, composed, and quietly capable: a focused instrument for link operations rather than a marketing surface or an enterprise dashboard.

## Anti-references

- Flashy crypto-style dashboards with neon gradients, glowing chrome, or decorative terminal effects.
- Dense enterprise admin panels that expose every control at once and make routine actions feel bureaucratic.
- Generic SaaS landing-page patterns, oversized marketing headlines, decorative metric cards, and glassmorphism.
- Novel controls that replace familiar form, table, navigation, and confirmation conventions without a functional reason.

## Design Principles

1. **Make the next action obvious.** Creation, search, editing, and link state must be understandable at a glance.
2. **Show trustworthy state.** Status, expiration, destination, and analytics should be unambiguous and updated close to the action that changed them.
3. **Keep power progressive.** Common tasks stay simple; advanced fields and destructive actions appear only when relevant.
4. **Prefer operational calm.** Dense data may be present, but hierarchy, spacing, and copy keep the interface quiet and scannable.
5. **Speed is part of the experience.** Server rendering, focused HTMX fragments, predictable navigation, and restrained motion must make the tool feel immediate.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The interface targets WCAG 2.2 AA. It must support keyboard-only operation, visible focus, semantic labels and status messages, sufficient contrast including placeholders and inactive text, color-independent status meaning, reduced-motion preferences, responsive layouts, and clear English error and confirmation copy.
